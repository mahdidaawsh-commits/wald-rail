Title: WaldRail: Consensus sequential reliability audit

WaldRail is an ordered GenLayer audit with exact sequential likelihood-ratio stopping. A deployment freezes trial IDs, commit-pinned report URLs and SHA-256 hashes. Callers cannot supply outcomes, skip trials or replace the plan. Leader and validators independently fetch complete reports and assess identity, finality, continuous duration, minimum flow and shutdown freedom. Validators require exact dimension agreement and audit source anchors. Valid PASS/FAIL observations update a reduced integer ratio; fixed inclusive bounds produce immutable CLEAR or FLAG. Incomplete reports block without changing the ratio; exhausted plans remain inconclusive. Tests compare arithmetic against an independent Fraction oracle, examine all five-dimension vectors and calculate capped-test error probabilities exactly. Statistical guarantees require a valid sampling model and honest, correctly classified observations; synthetic fixtures demonstrate execution only.
